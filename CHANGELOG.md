## [1.0.0]

The first version.

- A morning for each day of the week: its own time, and what opens – an app, a link (a video, playlist, album or film, in its own app) or a radio station played by the alarm itself. Steps chain.
- Spotify playlists and Yandex Music albums start playing at once; links shared from other apps go straight into a day.
- The portal: an arch onto six worlds drawn in code; the alarm is a synthesized hum with bells that rises slowly. Trace a circle to hold the portal open, then step through – or let a day open by itself.
- Finds: six per world, two rare; closing the day in the evening widens the portal, snoozing narrows it. Worlds level up, residents appear, the arch's frame is rebuilt.
- The morning journal: how waking up went, mornings at the first ring, which world wakes you best.
- Holidays, vacation, «don't wake me tomorrow», the wild portal from the «someday» list, «time to go» after the morning.
- «Will it ring?»: what the phone must allow, including Xiaomi's autostart.
- English and Russian.
